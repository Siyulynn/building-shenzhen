Disposable atlas milestone, not a Phase 1 feature and not a reconstruction of either built snapshot. The two existing assemblies keep their placements. Open the viewer with `?focus=core&layer=base` to see Shenzhenxu, the Luohu/Guomao corridor and Yumin in one view, or `?focus=core&layer=kh9&trace=1` to check the draft trace against the September 1980 image. Add `&basin=0` to hide the fabric.

**Photo observation overlay:** `kh9-observations.json` contains selected 1980 image traces and bilingual pins with explicit identification grades. In the local Vite viewer open `?focus=core&layer=kh9-core`; the portable build shows the same traces and pins on the existing `kh9` surface. This does not replace the illustrative 1979 or January 1984 geometry. The high-resolution raster remains local.

## Source intake / 参考资料

- **`phase1_shenzhenxu_1987_gazetteer_map`** — 《深圳市地名志》 plate, subject 1953–1978, `1:25,000`. This is the sheet supplied for the M3 review. It shows 宝安县, 深圳墟, 火车站, 罗湖山, 深圳河, 水稻田 and 鱼塘, and its legend separates building land, street, highway, pond, river and paddy. Recorded as tier D, `reference_only`: the copy is a reposted scan, and the plate is a period composite, not a survey of any one year. It guided *which* features to look for, not where they are; no scan pixels are embedded.
- **`phase1_shenzhenxu_1977_shenzhen_street_map`** — 《深圳镇街道图》, 1977, 广东省测绘局 / 惠阳地区革委会 / 宝安县革委会, `1:5,000`. The strongest pre-1979 geometry source in the register: one sheet carries 渔民村, 罗湖, 文锦渡, 深圳站, 宝安县/蔡屋围, 湖贝, the railway, the river and the pond groups. **Not yet georeferenced**, so this milestone only uses it to check topology.
- **`kh9_1980_09_11_a048`, segment c** — 11 September 1980, USGS KH-9 Declass 3. Loaded as `kh9_1980_slice.png` + `kh9_1980.json` from the existing M1c outputs. The crop is *sampled* at 6.25 m, not accurate to 6.25 m: the core's 2 km spatial-block holdout was 28.5 m against 1979 Landsat, and Landsat carries its own registration error. Transparent pixels and areas outside the crop fall back to relief.

The full eighteen-record intake is [`docs/research/shenzhenxu-map-register.md`](../../../../../docs/research/shenzhenxu-map-register.md).

## What the trace claims / 描绘内容

| Reference-map locator | September 1980 check | Atlas treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 「罗湖桥」 at the south end of the rail alignment; shoreline bends both sides | Rail, crossing and the large river bends are legible | Solid river and rail lines, one crossing marker. Widths and bank detail unresolved |
| Dense blocks east of the rail beside 「戏院」 | Dense texture and a crossing approach are discernible; named streets and compounds are not | Settlement fill plus dashed envelope, sized to the 1987 plate's ~900 × 700 m town. No parcel, theatre or bus-station footprint |
| The confluence and ponds west of the rail and station | Bends and several small dark enclosures are discernible; the tributary pick and pond edges need another look | Dashed Buji approach and pond-cluster candidate. The 1977 sheet puts 渔民村 on the north bank west of the rail; the houses are not re-placed yet |
| 「水稻田」 swath west of the rail | Lowland field pattern, not enough to infer crop or conversion dates | Four dashed lowland blocks with a generic paddy texture |
| 「罗湖山」 and station approach | Original elevations and grading dates cannot be recovered from the modern DEM or an undated contour sketch | No new hill, building footprint or Guomao siting |

`core-trace.json` holds the picks. **Solid** means visually legible on that image; **dashed** means interpretation. The source date is 1980 only. Claim IDs give context, not verification of the traced positions. The review window is `[363,365,412,398]` in grid-cell **edges**, picked at 24 review pixels per 100 m cell; `src/core.ts` converts to metres with the half-cell offset.

The fabric surfaces add art parameters on top of that geometry: river 52 m, Buji 34 m, rail 16 m, road 14 m. Widths, banks, plot lines and roof patterns are illustrative. The paddy and roofscape textures are generic canvas patterns with no surveyed boundaries.

## Limits and the next gate / 限制与下一步

1. **Improve the 1977 control set before re-tracing.** Seek the full sheet and a second set of independent picks. The Luohu bridge falls beyond the current crop, and the station throat and eastern road matches were not clear enough to pin down. Separate administrative overprint from transport, and keep new checks across Shenzhenxu, Yumin and Luohu/Wenjindu out of the fit. Where the map and KH-9 differ, record the gap rather than forcing an alignment.
2. **1984.** November 1984 MSS misses the core, and the footprint check also excludes the previously listed November 1983 KH-9 frames. Use separately dated photographs, records or plans for specific changes; otherwise leave January 1984 geometry inferred or unplaced.
3. **Shenzhenxu.** Between the 1917 schematic, the 1948 plan, the 1940/1934 sheets and the 1977 street sheet, the street names exist: 上大街, 下大街, 市場, 中山公園, 南塘村, 向南村, 戏院, 镇革委, 工人文化宫. That is enough for an **illustrative** block study — footprints `inferred`, never observed. That is M3.2.
4. **Fabric.** Building coverage is still the six study layouts. Basin-wide settlement massing, Hybrid facade materials and the continuous walk remain open.
5. **Do not display the scans.** Every map record is `reference_only`. They inform tracing; they do not become textures, overlays or viewer assets.
