# Municipal 3D atlas implementation / 深圳全市三维图集实施计划

**Scope:** isolated `3d-snapshots/snapshot-3d` atlas only. The Phase 1 playable slice remains 2D. The 931 × 686, 100 m grid is an approximately 93 × 69 km *rendering window*, including sea and neighbouring context. Its modern administrative mask is not a historical boundary.

## Review and decisions / 审查与决定

[The September 1980 generator](BASIN_GENERATOR.md) separates deterministic planning from Three.js meshes. The building and land kits, fit checks, focus masks and shared ground surface should carry forward. The browser review saw 16–18 draw calls for the core, not proof that the same density scales to the city. The core is currently one group, disappears from the municipality camera, and uses a dense 20 m lattice and about 66,000 instances for just 22.44 km². It has a sharp edge, a few conspicuous empty areas, wide embankments, and discontinuities at unresolved road crossings. The 20 m lattice originally interpolated the 100 m DEM; it was not a higher-detail source. The September 1980 scene, not the legacy 1979/84 kits, is the baseline.

The municipal view may render **close estimates for roads and railway known to exist**. Attach evidence for existence by epoch independently from the evidence for position. Estimate alignments with traced endpoints, dated route topology, visible reaches, valley/shoreline constraints and corroborated towns, stations or crossings, in that order. Modern maps can guide an unchanged reach only after cross-checking; do not substitute modern expressways, widened streets, double-track geometry or new bypasses. Show estimated geometry in a separate review layer, with a rationale and uncertainty/search band where defensible. If the route or its connection is not supported, keep it unresolved rather than completing a network for appearance.

## Steps / 实施步骤

1. **Core polish / 核心区完善.** Wire the handoff's registered 30 m *modern* surface patch into the lattice, keeping identical edge heights. Use smoother relief shading and low-contrast illustrative cover on unclassified ground (no extra settlement, field parcels or historical crop claims). Reconcile water widths, bridge spans and exclusion buffers using the sampled bank windows as local guidance, not surveyed widths. Where two approaches and a crossing are credible, connect them in the **render-only** model with an estimated deck or level-crossing treatment and preserve the unresolved evidence. Keep remaining gaps explicit. Review matched core, yard and collar views; do not move the legacy kits.
2. **Municipal GIS and estimated network / 全市底图与估计交通网.** Prepare a versioned EPSG:32650 handoff in this order:
   - Register the regional 25 m KH‑9 overview and 6.25 m slice with their actual validity masks, the 1977 Bao'an sheet as a route/topology guide, and the October 1979 MSS data as a broad water/coast comparator. Analyse the modern DEM for plausible relief/gradients, not dates. Publish coverage by sector rather than assuming the imagery covers the modern municipality evenly.
   - Start with the documented KCR through route (`site.kcr_line.exists_1979`): join the core `rail-main` endpoints and follow the period railway evidence through Sungang and Buji toward Pinghu and the northern continuation; render a Lo Wu approach as context. Map the Guangzhou–Shenzhen road (`site.guangshen_road.exists_1979`) separately from Shennan (`phase1.map.shennan_1980`, `phase1.shennan.first_paved_segment_west`). Start west through Nantou and the Bao'an lowlands. Collect dated support for other eastern/northeastern trunks and secondary spurs before assigning historic names and dates. Do not make Shennan continuous by January 1984 merely because a later alignment is known.
   - Establish a coastal and water-mask workflow: separate known sea, likely intertidal, inland water and no data. Prevent modern fill from leaking into the old coast; the existing broad coast-change count is a candidate, not a measured reclaimed area. Treat inland reservoirs separately from the lowland coastline (a lowland cutoff would omit genuine upland water). Start with the Shenzhen Reservoir (`site.shenzhen_reservoir.exists_1979`); don't generate dams or name waterways from the DEM alone.
   - Add broad settlement envelopes and neutral rural context. Prioritise Nantou, Shekou/Chiwan, Sungang and the Luohu–reservoir collar; extend inland/east after coverage checks. Separate KH‑9-visible built extent, interpreted cover and art-only massing. Unknown terrain may have subtle texture, not invented named settlements or sectors of asserted paddy.
   - Deliver source locators and dates, stable segment and anchor IDs, parent corridor IDs, evidence and render status, reason for each estimate, joined crossings/portals and unresolved parts. Check shared endpoints, water and terrain constraints, no accidental road/rail junctions, and no unsupported boundary crossings. Keep the core source and existing Phase 1 site register read-only.
3. **LOD0 municipal view / 市域远景.** Ship a coarse relief, dated shoreline and major-water sheet, simplified transport ribbons, and a few basin/settlement super-batches; no individual small trees or bunds and no citywide shadows. Start with the protocol's ≤16 draw-call target and profile on a recorded device.
4. **LOD1 neighbourhood chunks / 街区分块.** Use regular 3.2 km chunks and cull out-of-view batches. Compile network connectivity and long profiles across the whole map before clipping to chunks. Share seam vertices, choose one owner for structures crossing a tile edge, and seed by feature ID and world tile. Cache neighbouring chunks and test load/unload, pan, frame time and memory on a short core-to-Nantou and core-to-Sungang corridor before wider rollout. The initial neighbourhood budget is ≤120 draw calls, not a measured frame-rate guarantee.
   - **4B — Harden the pilot / 加固试点.** Keep tile preparation off the main thread, retain LOD0 during loading, and test transitions, cache retention, and the core collar without adding pilot corridors.
   - **4C — Extend review coverage / 扩展审阅范围.** Add the separable October 1979 reservoir components and conservative coastal fabric pockets. Screen both northern rail bands against the 30 m modern surface without using it as period alignment evidence.
5. **Epochs and LOD2 / 时点与精模.** Apply sourced additions, removals, changes to stage and explicit continuity assumptions from September 1980 back to the 1979 opening and forward to January 1984. Keep photo observations separately selectable. Replace focus kits only after matching dated photos and anchors (Shenzhenxu frontage, Yumin, Luohu/Guomao, then other qualified areas). Do not treat November 1984 MSS or a year-only 1984 photo as the January cutoff. Validate portable output without the private scans.
   - **5A — Shenzhenxu frontage prototype / 深圳墟临街原型.** One local crossroads and two cameras first. Separate visible architectural cues from dating, metric geometry and building identification before upgrading any bookend kit.

    - **5B — Municipal bookends / 市域前后时点。** Derive both views from the September baseline; use December 1977 KH‑9 only where supported. Integrate unnamed Shenzhenxu continuity and January stage models with the streamed core.
