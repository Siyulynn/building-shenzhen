# The 1981 municipal government building / 1981年市政府大楼

Research note for the 1984 film frame labelled **深圳市政府大楼** and for the memoir passage reproduced in a 2025 satellite-imagery blog. The building is not in the slice yet. This note fixes what can be said, what is inference, and what a later landmark, encyclopedia entry or walkable route would still need.

## Short answer

**It is the six-storey Party and government office block opened in 1981 in the 市委大院 on 深南中路, Futian.** The 1982 city map labels the compound 深圳市人民政府 and the hill beside it 上步岭; the September 1980 KH-9 frame shows the site before construction. It is not the 市民中心 (opened 2004), not the old Bao'an county offices, and not the 市府二办 on 上步中路.

- **Where.** North side of 深南中路, in the western part of the block bounded by 深南中路 (south), 红荔路 (north), 上步中路 (west) and 红岭中路 (east) — today's 通新岭社区. The compound gate faces 深南中路; 荔枝公园 occupies the eastern part of the same block. The cadre dormitories were on the slope directly behind (north), at 通新岭.
- **What it looked like.** A long, low horizontal slab of **six storeys**, white or light concrete, with continuous bands of windows on each floor and a flat roof. In front, a very wide flat entrance portal carried on columns, over the drive; a low boundary wall, hedge and flower beds; cars parked in the forecourt. A lower two-storey wing sat at the right-hand end. Film and photographs show the same family of forms from 1984 to 2010.
- **Confidence.** The compound is documented: the 1982 map labels it, the 2010 photographs fix its frontage, and the 1980 frame shows it empty. The identification of the 1984 frame with this block is strong but not yet closed, because no source names the street in the same sentence as the building. The exact footprint, floor area, architect and the block's present condition are open.

The 2004 report is the cleanest statement that the old **市政府** sat on 深南中路 and shared a compound with the **市委**: "深南中路的深圳市政府，这里是深圳市委、市政府领导以及人事局、纪委等部门的办公点……这里将作为深圳市委的办公地，深圳市委、市政府合署办公的历史将结束。" It also calls the old offices "20多年办公历史", which fits a 1981–82 build without dating it.

The 1982 map adds the missing period label: on the photographed sheet the compound sits east of 上步路 and north of 深南路, with **市委宿舍** immediately north and **上步岭** immediately east. The sheet also labels the 科学馆, which may still have been planned in 1982, so treat its blocks as schematic. Zhang Xunfu's recollection supplies the decision (a site beside the lychee orchard, with the low ground turned into a lake), and 丘盘连's memoir independently puts the **市委大楼** at the end of 1981, 200 m west of the newspaper's planned site near 深南大道. The 1980 KH-9 crop supplies the negative: no large building in September 1980.

## Locating it

The chain, and where each link is weak:

1. **The memoir puts the office on 上埗岭 with dorms behind at 通心岭.** It gives no street. "上埗岭" is not yet matched to a gazetteer entry; it reads as the local name for the rise between 深南中路 and 红荔路.
2. **The address evidence puts the 市委大院 at 深南中路1008号.** The 2017 notice is a real official document, but it is a 2017 address. Later listings give **1018号** for the same gate (the 2024 historic-building entry for the 《艰苦岁月》 sculpture says "深南中路1018号市委大门西侧围墙外"). The 10-number difference is unresolved; it does not move the site.
3. **Present-day street geometry closes the block.** OSM puts 通新岭社区 between 深南中路 (about 22.5434 N), 红荔路 (about 22.5519 N), 上步中路 (about 114.0911 E) and 红岭中路 (about 114.0996 E). 荔枝公园 occupies the eastern part of that block, from about 114.0936 E. The compound therefore sits in the western strip, roughly **22.544 N, 114.092–114.093 E**, with 通新岭 housing on the slope directly north. The blog's "dorms behind the office" and "office on the hill" fit this block and no other nearby one.
4. **Two dated photographs fix the frontage.** The 2010 views of the gate are geotagged at **22.54346 N, 114.09347 E** and **22.54348 N, 114.09310 E** — camera positions on the south side of 深南中路, looking north. That puts the north-side gate at about **114.0933 E**, within a few dozen metres of the park's west edge, and the office block behind it at about **22.544 N**. `phase1.site.municipal_government.gate_coords_2010`.
5. **The 1982 city map labels the block.** The photographed 1982 深圳市区图 marks **深圳市人民政府** east of 上步路 and north of 深南路, draws a building block inside the compound, puts **市委宿舍** immediately north, and names the hill immediately east **上步岭**. That turns the inference into a documented site and supplies the name in the memoir. `phase1.map.1982.municipal_government_compound`, `phase1.map.1982.shangbuling`. Caveats: it is a phone photo with no scale or imprint, and it also labels the 科学馆, which may still have been planned in 1982, so it may mix built and proposed features. It fixes relative position, not a footprint or a completion date.
6. **The 1980 frame shows the site before construction.** The registered KH-9 frame of 11 September 1980 shows the block as cultivation, trees and small structures, with 深南路 along its south edge — no large building and no cleared pad. At 7.5 m per pixel it can rule out a finished block, not small works. `phase1.observation.kh9_1980.municipal_site_unbuilt`. That is consistent with a 1981 build.
7. **The 1984 frame is the remaining inference.** Its caption says 市政府大楼, the 1981 and 1986 captions say 市委大楼 / 市委办公大楼, and the memoir says both organs shared one building. If those are the same block, the frame is the 深南中路 building. No single source says so; treat the identity as a strong lead (`phase1.site.municipal_government.1981_location`).

**Do not confuse the compounds.** The 2004 report names **一办** and **二办**. 一办 is the 深南中路 / 市委大院 site. 二办 is a separate compound on 上步中路 (No. 1023 on the 市委统战部 contact page, seen as a search summary, tier E). The 1981 building belongs to the first.

## How it looked

**1984 film frame** (green-tinted, low resolution). The camera looks north across a forecourt. A long white slab of six storeys runs across the frame, with continuous horizontal window bands and a flat roof. A wide, flat horizontal element on columns spans the drive in front — either a canopy attached to the block or the compound gate; the frame cannot decide. A lower two-storey wing closes the right-hand end. In front: a low wall, clipped hedge, flower beds, and cars and a van parked on the forecourt. There is no portico tower, no glass curtain wall and no visible ornament beyond the horizontal bands. Nothing measured: no bay spacing, floor height or length can be read off this frame.

**1986 photograph** (caption only, not inspected). The 市委办公大楼 with the 孺子牛 sculpture in front. This ties the building to the sculpture that the 2004 report places at the 一办 gate.

**2010 photograph** (public domain, inspected). Looking north from 深南中路: a white gate portal with a flat roof and slim columns, red flags on the roof, a low white boundary wall, planting, and the 孺子牛 behind the gate. Above and behind the gate, partly screened by trees, a six-storey white slab with a continuous top-floor window band. This is consistent with the 1984 slab and is the best evidence that a six-storey block still stood in 2010 — but the trees hide the lower floors and the photograph cannot prove the 1984 block survived rather than being replaced by a similar one.

**Against the 2020 市民中心** (as the video does): the 1981 building is a single slab in a walled, planted compound on a street frontage; the 市民中心 is a 435 m long complex under a blue roof, opened in 2004, with no perimeter wall. The contrast the video draws is real, but it compares a government office block with a civic complex, not two versions of the same building type.

## Reliability notes

- **The memoir is at second hand.** The blog quotes 《文化深圳》 without a page; the WeChat original was not opened. "1981年年底" is late December, but the claim keeps year precision. A move date is an anchor-date use and needs a second independent source.
- **Construction vs occupation.** The 1981 photo caption says "建设中"; the blog's memoir says the move happened at the end of 1981; Zhang Xunfu recalls people "住人和办公" by early 1981, but his sentence is about the cadre housing, not the office block. The three can be reconciled as housing first, office block later in the year, but no source says so. Do not turn "1981" into a single date. The tension is recorded in [`conflict.phase1.municipal_government.occupation_date`](../../data/evidence/conflicts/phase1.municipal_government.occupation_date.yaml).
- **The site was empty in September 1980.** The KH-9 crop is the only dated image of the block before construction, and it is a lead: the registration is good to about 28 m and the pixels are 7.5 m. It rules out a finished building, not site preparation.
- **Two labels, one building?** The 1984 film says 市政府大楼; the 1981 and 1986 captions say 市委大楼 / 市委办公大楼; the memoir says the Party and government shared the block. This is a labelling difference, not a conflict about the fabric, but it is why the film cannot be used alone to identify the site.
- **Survival is a lead.** The 2010 photo and the 2017 "前楼" reference suggest the block survived into the 2010s. A 2024 street-level post, seen only as a search summary (tier E), describes the compound as "简简单单的几座低层办公楼". Neither is a demolition or renovation record. `phase1.site.municipal_government.1981_survival` stays a lead.
- **The address number is not settled.** 1008 (2017 notice) vs 1018 (later listings). Record both; do not average or silently pick one.

## Open questions

1. The compound plan and the block's footprint, floor area and orientation. The 1982 map is a schematic photo, not a survey; no plan or flat scan has been opened.
2. Construction start, topping-out and occupation dates, and the design institute or architect.
3. Whether the 1981 block is the **前楼** named in 2017, and whether it still stands in 2026.
4. The relation between 一办 (深南中路) and 二办 (上步中路1023号): which departments sat where in 1981–2003.
5. Where the Party and government offices were before the move (the memoir mentions 新园招待所 in 罗湖; the 1979 county seat was at 蔡屋围). A separate line of research.
6. Whether the five blocks on the 1980 hill are the same five dormitory blocks the memoir describes, which would push part of 通新岭 back before the office building.
