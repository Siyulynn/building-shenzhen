# Shenzhen Phase 1 research · 1979–1984

Research brief for the Luohu–Shekou playable slice. Evidence records are in `data/evidence/claims/phase1_statistics.yaml`, `phase1_findings.yaml` and `phase1_m2_m4.yaml`. They are ready for draft use with the project's evidence labels. Preserve each statistical vintage and geographic unit in calibration reports.

## What to build from this

Use **1979-Q1 through 1984-Q4** as the research window. The slice spec (v0.3) plays **1979-Q1 to 1984-Q1**, 21 quarters:
- a six-quarter prologue to 1980-Q2;
- the SEZ in 1980-Q3;
- the close at the January 1984 visit.

Its final state is end-1983, so the closing calibration uses end-1983 figures. 1984 figures inform the grant cliff and later phases. The March city decision and the Shekou works happen in play. The 1979 year-end population belongs at the first December checkpoint. The January seed needs its own estimate or a geographically comparable end-1978 figure. ### Budget, investors and foreign exchange

- **The strongest fiscal mechanic is the end of the provincial grant.** The municipal finance bureau says Shenzhen retained the revenue covered by the arrangement, received provincial funding tied to a 1978 expenditure base, kept that funding level in 1982, received half in 1983 and took the expense onto its own retained revenues from 1984. Start with a labeled placeholder for the amount of the base. In 1983 warn the player that recurring commitments may outlast their funding. The same account describes district fixed grants with retained overperformance and no automatic bailout for shortfalls. [1985 SEZ Yearbook fiscal chapter, pp. 469–470](https://www.shenzhenshizhi.cn/book/8732df747f9b9aa1c47cfff5ea30c585/财政收支状况与体制改革_126.pdf)
- **Issue a project loan and make co-financing a distinct option.** The finance bureau reports ¥570m of cumulative bank loans guaranteed by the fiscal authorities for development infrastructure in 1979–84. This measures lending over six years. For scale, the 2014 yearbook shows citywide public-budget revenue/expenditure of ¥17.21m/¥29.71m in 1979 and ¥294.35m/¥279.54m in 1984. The period account gives ¥509.28m of broad fiscal spending for 1984, covering a different presentation. The early broad revenue table has merged OCR columns, and the narrative revenue total, increase and growth statements need reconciliation against the scan. Keep the broad revenue series out of target data until that check. [Period finance chapter](https://www.shenzhenshizhi.cn/book/8732df747f9b9aa1c47cfff5ea30c585/财政收支状况与体制改革_126.pdf) · [Period fiscal table](https://www.shenzhenshizhi.cn/book/8732df747f9b9aa1c47cfff5ea30c585/财政金融统计_169.pdf) · [2014 yearbook table 1-3, continuation 2](https://www.sz.gov.cn/attachment/0/848/848512/9148002.pdf)
- **A lease is a negotiated use certificate with fees.** The 1981 rules took effect on 1982-01-01. Articles 15–18 give use-specific terms, statutory fee ranges and payment options. Industrial fees are ¥10–30/m²/year on the schedule. Actual collections can differ because of negotiation and waivers. Keep the 1987 auction mechanic outside the slice. The 1980 SEZ rules already gave covered enterprises a 15% enterprise income tax rate and contemplated extra preferences for specified investments. [Land rules, arts. 5, 8, 15–18 and 23](https://www.shenzhenshizhi.cn/book/8732df747f9b9aa1c47cfff5ea30c585/深圳经济特区土地管理暂行规定_40.pdf) · [SEZ regulations, arts. 12–14](https://www.hengqin.gov.cn/lab/flfg/fl/content/post_3512255.html)
- **Make the Hong Kong offer legible.** A processing contract (来料加工) supplies equipment or materials and an order. The local counterparty supplies premises and labour and earns an agreed processing fee. Give cards distinct fields for the gross value shipped, processing fee, foreign funds actually deployed, local taxable income and any quota to buy forex. Citywide foreign funds actually used in the period account grew from HK$1.13334bn in 1983 to HK$1.65747bn in 1984. Hong Kong represented 90.3% of contracted capital over 1979–84. Keep the 1978 predecessor trade in the backstory and call the milestone **Our first processing contract / 本市在本局中的首单来料加工** or tie it explicitly to the player's site. [Processing history](https://www.szdag.gov.cn/dawh/tqssn/content/post_930475.html) · [Foreign investment chapter](https://www.shenzhenshizhi.cn/book/8732df747f9b9aa1c47cfff5ea30c585/引进外资情况_17.pdf)
- **Use an official import allocation plus the RMB price in Phase 1.** National foreign-exchange retention started in 1979. Shenzhen's 1985 local swap rules appear in a later repeal list. The opening municipal allocation, enterprise sharing rates, and the effective rates paid on specific 1979–84 equipment transactions remain open. Treat Hong Kong trade stats as a broad external-demand proxy and keep the large early jump in Shenzhen's separate export series under review. [SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2018/1109/10674.html) · [Gazette repeal list, items 44 and 61](https://www.sz.gov.cn/zfgb/2003/gb327/content/post_5000124.html) · [Hong Kong 1985 Digest of Statistics](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/data/stat_report/product/B1010003/att/B10100031985AN85E0100.pdf)
