Match the Dongmen grade crossing to the traced rail alignment before using nearby street views to locate block edges. Album panoramas include later digital stitches of period exposures. 1. **Shenzhenxu crossroads / 深圳墟十字街口.** Fit R01 to the two traced road axes. Compare the frontages across the 1983 and 1985 references before assigning epoch-specific shops.
2. **Caiwuwei road bend / 蔡屋围转弯处.** Recreate Jiang's bend composition, then compare road edges in the two 1982 directional views.
3. **Guomao from Guoshang / 国商望国贸.** Anchor both sites independently. Use the January visit and February exterior as separate dates, with January tower height open.
