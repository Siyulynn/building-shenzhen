深圳百科 research index / 深圳百科资料索引

A parsed, sorted finding aid for szbaike.com. It indexes the site’s article catalogue, extracts the articles most useful for the 1979–2009 city and the M3 core basin, and lists candidate factual sentences for human checking. The site is a compilation with mixed provenance; every row is a lead, not a verified fact.
