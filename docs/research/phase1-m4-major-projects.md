# Shennan Road, Guomao and the early public works / 深南路、国贸与早期公共工程

Research for the M4 project ledger and the 1979–January 1984 playable arc. The [evidence register](../../data/evidence/claims/phase1_m4_projects.yaml) holds the dated milestones and tracked discrepancies. Project proposals, funding decisions, work on site and delivery are separate stages.

For starting construction capacity, outside contractors, site servicing and village self-build, see [Early construction capacity](phase1-m4-construction-capacity.md).

## Shennan Road and the Lufeng crews / 深南路与陆丰筑路工

**The first job was a road through existing country.** By 1979, Shenzhen relied on the old 107 road for access toward Guangzhou. Former planner Yu Wanjun recalls that Lufeng County's Shenzhen office called on home communities to send **600 construction workers**. In a separate interview, Luo Zhijiang describes Luoxi Commune, then part of Lufeng, sending a contingent of **more than 300**. He recalls bringing hand tools, living in temporary sheds near Badeng village, cutting hills and tamping the roadbed. He dates his arrival to after the 1979 Spring Festival. Preserve the larger and smaller scopes separately. There is no common roll or same-day headcount. `phase1.shennan.lufeng_crew`, `phase1.shennan.luoxi_arrival`, `phase1.shennan.badeng_sheds`. [Yu interview](https://news.sina.com.cn/o/2005-07-16/06086447981s.shtml) · [Luo interview and photographs](https://www.dutenews.com/n/article/9000156)

| Date | Evidence | M4 use |
|---|---|---|

The municipal archive also mentions extensions toward Huangbeiling and Shanghai Hotel in the same description. `phase1.map.shennan_1980`, `phase1.shennan.first_paved_segment_west`, `phase1.shennan.first_paved_segment_archive_limits`. | Improve a short, existing corridor. ## Guomao, the landmark under construction / 国贸大厦

Use **深圳国际贸易中心大厦 / Shenzhen International Trade Centre (Guomao / 国贸)** as M4's major project. Its in-slice payoff is a visibly rising tower and a commitment the city still has to finance. The January 1984 visit can show the works from **国际商业大厦 / International Commercial Building (Guoshang / 国商)** across the way. Guoshang was a separate Hong Kong cooperation project, completed in April 1983 in Gu Mu's account, and already offers a scale and procurement comparison. `phase1.guomao.construction_visible_january_visit`, `phase1.guoshang.commercial_building_completed`. [Gu Mu's recollection](https://www.zywxpress.com/c/2021-02-25/1350282.shtml)

| Milestone | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **1981 · Assemble the project** | In May the city decided on pooled financing. The archive exhibit records fundraising in June and the design tender in July. The purpose was shared office and commercial space for organizations establishing a Shenzhen presence. `phase1.guomao.collective_finance_decision`. [Archive exhibit](https://www.szdag.gov.cn/gzdt/gzdt/content/post_685369.html) |
| **1982 · Break ground and lay piles** | The archive narrative gives April for breaking ground. Gu Mu dates piling from May to October. The exhibit dates formal work to May. | **1983 · Rise above ground** | A May report describes the foundations as recently laid. Gu Mu dates standard-floor work to August. The municipal archive places the slipform trials in June–October. | **After the slice · Top out and deliver** | The main tower topped out **30 April 1984**. The archive dates building completion to **29 December 1985** and reports approximately **100,000 m²** of floor. `phase1.guomao.main_tower_topped`, `phase1.event.guo_mao_completion`, `phase1.guomao.completed_floor`. [Archive exhibit](https://www.szdag.gov.cn/gzdt/gzdt/content/post_685369.html) |

The project preview can show current commitments, committed outside funds, cash still needed, and the cost of deferring other works. Record any city share, payments and recurring obligations separately. The original partner contributions and construction payment schedule remain open. Historical progress by January is a useful comparison for a funded run. A completed tower or its eventual operating income would put the outcome beyond the historical slice.

## The Eight Major Cultural Facilities / 八大文化设施

Seven projects recur across the consulted versions. **图书馆 / library, 博物馆 / museum, 电视台 / TV station, 体育馆 / gymnasium, 大剧院 / grand theatre, 科学馆 or 科技馆 / science hall, 新闻文化中心 / news and culture centre.** The eighth changes by source. Tan Siying recalls **深圳特区报 / Shenzhen Special Zone Daily** in the 1982 priorities. Hu Hongxia's roster gives **美术馆 / art museum**. A 1993 People's Daily account includes **深圳大学 / Shenzhen University**. Track the university explicitly even when presenting an early roster that uses another eighth entry. `phase1.culture.roster.tan_1982`, `phase1.culture.roster.hu_1982`, `phase1.culture.roster.peoples_daily_1993`. [Tan interview](https://www.szzx.gov.cn/szzx/zxzt/szkss/content/post_1239034.html) · [Hu essay](https://www.sohu.com/a/567368264_118622) · [1993 People's Daily](https://cn.govopendata.com/renminribao/1993/08/14/3/)

**Capital scale.** The 1985 yearbook gives a **¥160m three-year plan from 1982**. Hu's essay gives **¥700m** with unclear timing and scope. A contemporary April 1984 report gives **more than ¥63.45m actually invested from 1979 through 1983** across local fiscal capital spending on culture, education, health, sport and science, or **33.17%** of the stated appropriation total. That denominator and time window differ from the programme estimates. Use the period project figures for draft cost comparisons and the contemporary amount as broad fiscal context. `phase1.culture.capital_programme.yearbook`, `phase1.culture.capital_programme.hu`, `phase1.culture.actual_local_capex_through_1983`. ### Shenzhen University / 深圳大学

The university archive dates the preparatory committee to **14 January 1983**, selection of **1 km² at Gui Miao by Houhai Bay** to **27 March**, and the Ministry of Education's approval notice to **10 May**. The first opening ceremony took place at **Shenzhen Theatre on 27 September**. The city used former Bao'an county offices for temporary premises. Permanent campus Phase 1 broke ground on **7 February 1984**, shortly after the playable visit. `phase1.szu.preparing_committee`, `phase1.szu.site_and_temporary_premises`, `phase1.szu.approval_notice`, `phase1.szu.first_opening_ceremony`, `phase1.szu.permanent_campus.ground_break`. [University archive chronology](https://archives.szu.edu.cn/shenzhendaxuedashijihuibian1983-1996.pdf)

The in-slice sequence is a citywide commitment, temporary classrooms and staffing, followed by the first intake. The permanent campus belongs to the western planning area outside the playable grid. Represent it in M4 as a budget and project decision visible by card. The dated September meeting entry says **close to ¥100m** in campus capital, while a later account gives **¥50m**. The [allocation conflict](../../data/evidence/conflicts/phase1.szu.founding_allocation.yaml) keeps both and leaves the actual payment schedule open. [University archive chronology](https://archives.szu.edu.cn/shenzhendaxuedashijihuibian1983-1996.pdf) · [Retrospective](https://www.sznews.com/news/content/2018-05/14/content_19098098_0.htm)
