The early-reform recollection gives a practical anchor for Area 1. **十字街 / Crossroads** was the meeting of east–west 解放路 / Jiefang Road and north–south 人民路 / Renmin Road. 南塘菜市场 / Nantang Vegetable Market lay south in the lane network, and the author recalls the theatre, shops and inns around the core. ## Zone purpose and investor siting / 用地分类与投资选址

A May 1983 report names industrial districts, 港仓区 / port and warehouse areas, 商业区 / commercial areas, 住宅群 / housing groups and 公共建筑 / public buildings, with much of the work still ahead. A retrospective puts ten industrial districts in the 1982 outline. Use these as plan categories and scale context. The plan date, start of works, first delivered building and operating stock are distinct milestones. `phase1.zoning.plan_categories_1983`, `phase1.zoning.industrial_districts_planned.1982`. [1983 People's Daily](https://cn.govopendata.com/renminribao/1983/05/06/5/) · [1982 outline retrospective](https://static.nfnews.com/content/202507/21/c11532070.html?enterColumnId=6866)

| M3a use | How to read the evidence in M3b |
|---|---|
| 工业 / Industrial | Classify by the project's main activity. The period yearbook describes factories with warehouses, switching stations, shops and staff dormitories. Record these as supporting facilities, jobs or beds where the investor model supports them. Freight-oriented industrial storage can use `industrial` as a draft mapping if it becomes playable. `phase1.zoning.industrial_supporting_uses`. |
| 商业 / Commercial | Separate existing trading streets from new planned shopping sites. Luohu's Friendship Department Store and the Overseas Chinese Goods Store provide in-window examples of commercial investment. `phase1.zoning.friendship_store.formed`, `phase1.zoning.overseas_chinese_store.opened`. |
| 住宅 / Residential | Use for planned housing sites. Record existing village houses, rooms and company dormitories as housing stock. Industrial projects can create demand for beds and provide dormitories within the project. `phase1.zoning.industrial_supporting_uses`. |
| 公共 / Public | Use for dedicated civic and service sites. Keep site presence, frontage access and actual service capacity distinct. `phase1.zoning.plan_categories_1983`. |
| 预留 / Reserve | Treat as a player plan choice. Existing fields, waterways and hills belong to the starting land and buildability layers. A historic reserve designation needs its own plan evidence. |

For candidate ordering, first check the required use and count the cells that are playable, buildable, serviced and available to the project. Keep candidate size, access and capacity available to the investor preview. M3a's greedy sort ranks **names** by overlap, offset and scale with deterministic ties. Use site capacity and access for investment siting. Commercial and industrial investor siting already has an entry point in the slice spec. Record logistics and mixed-use evidence under the existing zoning types.
