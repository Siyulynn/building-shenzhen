# Historical Imagery Coverage / 历史影像覆盖

**Scope.** Imagery and survey evidence for the 1979 opening and January 1984 M0–M2 scenes. The December 1977 KH-9 scans are the strongest pre-opening aerial evidence in hand. One segment supports comparisons in the core basin. January 1984 still lacks a dated, detailed view across the core and west. Use the 1977 scan to constrain the opening scene and target late-1983 or January-1984 photography and surveys for the second scene.

**Update, 25 November 2026.** The Hong Kong flight index has been screened against the core. It names a 22 December 1983 flight over the frontier, high-resolution 1976 and 1978 runs, and confirms that no 1984 flight reaches the mainland; every frame that shows more than the river strip is restricted. Details in [hk-flight-candidates.md](hk-flight-candidates.md). The Shenzhen photo catalogue was downloaded in full; rights stay unrecorded.
