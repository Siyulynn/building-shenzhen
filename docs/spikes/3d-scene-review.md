### Catalogue sweep, 1979–85

The public catalogue was re-read in full on 26 November 2026: **527 entries** listed across the district and
subject pages, **190** carrying a 1979–85 year in the caption, and **78** frames cached for this review. The
register adds cameras for Luohu's 1980–84 works, the 1985 Caiwuwei/Shifu/Heping oblique aerials, Nantou,
Bagualing, the Shekou and Chiwan harbours, Mawan, Liantang, the two border crossings and the 1979–80 street
views. Portraits, ceremonies and factory interiors were left out. The undated frames that show Guomao rising
are included as construction-era views; the undated frames that show it finished are listed but flagged as
later.

Three caption faults matter for future work. `954` 渔民村1979 serves a bulldozer scene, `1453` 东湖丽苑（1981）
serves a present-day photograph, and `957`/`958` duplicate the 1984 Nantou and 1985 Guomao frames already in
the set. No model or map geometry was changed in this sweep; the only scene edit is the longer aerial fog
distance and the new camera list.

## Open-question follow-up · 遗留问题补充 (2026-11-25)

The main evidence store now carries a focused pass on the atlas's open checks. It adds no surveyed footprint and changes no scene geometry.

- **Shekou:** seven village names, the pre-1979 fishing character, the 1947 market hamlet, and the typhoon shelter between 蛇口山 and the coast south of it. The shelter was expanded to 19,000 m² in 1953 and later to 53,000 m²; its site became 蛇口广场 after 2000. The modern 蛇口渔港 is therefore not a safe proxy for the historical harbour.
- **Nantou:** a measured walled extent of about 680 × 500 m (roughly 70,000 m²), nine historical street names, eight streets surviving today, more than 40 Qing and Republican houses, and wall-brick removal in the early reform period. The 1979 wall condition and street widths remain open.
- **Northern rail:** the 1911 station sequence, a February 1962 station between Shenzhen and Buji, its 1977 rename to 深圳北站 as the main freight station, and a May 1979 timetable with 25 intermediate stations. Existence and station sequence are supported; the 1979 alignment and yard boundary are not.
- **Yumin:** a second contemporary report of 35 villas with drains, concrete lanes, courtyard gardens and goldfish ponds; an oral history of shore huts, 1962 flat-roofed houses and a fall from more than 70 households to about 30; and a 2018 feature pairing 35 households with 33 buildings. The 33/35 count and the 1,500,000/770,000-yuan cost are open conflicts.

See [map sources §7](../research/phase1-map-sources.md#7-3d-atlas-follow-up-2026-11-25--三维图集遗留问题补充) and [`phase1_atlas_open_questions.yaml`](../../data/evidence/claims/phase1_atlas_open_questions.yaml). The two atlas photo claims and the `atlas_szlzp_catalogue` source are now in `data/evidence/`; the image files remain reference-only and do not ship.

## Handoff to the 3D build

The [1984 public previews and 1979 anchor review](../research/map-1984-previews-and-1979-anchors.md#snapshot-handoff) gives the existing 1979 and 24 January 1984 scenes a locality-level comparison. In particular, the Caiwuwei detail says `立体交叉（未建成）` (`phase1.map.1984.caiwuwei_grade_separation_unbuilt`); treat it as a check against a finished interchange in the January scene.

Use the [3D modelling protocol](../design/3d-modelling-protocol.md) as the contract. Keep the two views of the evidence separate: **municipality-wide context** (coarse terrain, dated or explicitly provisional water, settlement masses) and **focused-area detail** (start with Yumin and its field edge in Luohu; add other areas only when their inputs are ready). The [1979 slice start](../../content/slice/start_1979.yaml) is not a citywide building inventory; the [end-1983 checkpoint](../../content/slice/checkpoint_1983q4.yaml) gives calibration targets, not parcel locations. Use end-1983 as the scene state for the opening of 1984-Q1, rather than reading a November 1984 image as the January city. Where the core lacks period coverage, mark geometry inferred or leave it absent.

Before these scenes become snapshot inputs, confirm the intended time cutoffs, attach evidence locators to every observed geometry layer, and keep illustrative buildings, plots and weather out of the historical evidence layer. The next meaningful checks are real-input city and focused-area comparisons, then one continuous source-checked Yumin walk and a timed authoring pass. This review viewer does not change Phase 1 scope or the other session’s build.
